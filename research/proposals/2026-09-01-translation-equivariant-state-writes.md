## Claim and Research Question

A fixed-size recurrent state that stores meaning should write the same thing for two translations of the same span. The object is the per-head **pure write** of a Gated DeltaNet (GDN) layer over a span `a` after a shared prefix `c`, `W(a|c) = S(c⊕a) − S_{v=0}(c,a)`, where `S_{v=0}(c,a)` is the state after running the span from `S(c)` with the value inputs zeroed (decay and erase kept, writes removed). Wave 2 supervised the raw segment delta `D = S(c⊕a) − S(c)`; both wave-2 judges showed that `D` carries a content-independent prefix term `(∏α − 1)S(c)` shared by every translation pair, so the old loss was satisfiable through prefix identity. Wave 3 replaced the object, the negatives, the control and the phase-0 gates so that this shortcut is impossible by construction. Wave 4 makes the pilot readable and runnable: a pre-registered phase-1 instrument-sensitivity gate (so an all-arms-at-floor grid is read as "instrument at floor", not "write adds nothing"), a W-vs-P distinctness floor (so the decisive control cannot be the treatment by construction), per-arm λ selection, and a registered GDN hybrid as the phase-0 substrate so every pre-registered kill has a runnable arm. Wave 5 turns the CPU object doctor from prose into executable code (`harness/translation_equivariant_state_writes.py`, `scripts/run_translation_equivariant_state_writes_doctor.py`, receipt `data/results/translation-equivariant-state-writes/phase0-doctor.json`; synthetic-case numbers only), re-derives the phase-1 promotion and kill rule by simulation, and resyncs the substrate, image, receipt and parallel-data statements to the repository state.

Research question, two phases:

- **Phase 0 (frozen, within-model kill screen).** On the registered and receipted `qwen3.5-4b-base` (`Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B-Base`, revision `1001bb4d826a52d1f399e183466143f4da7b741b`, claim C39 — the base sibling of the 24 GDN + 8 full-attention `Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B` of claim C32, so the wave-4 post-training confound is removed; its own config.json is read in the container smoke), does the GDN state carry cross-lingual recall at all (D-zero vs a matched distractor-span perturbation), is the cross-lingual gap a storage-format effect rather than readout interference (N-scaling contrast at N in {8, 32, 128}), and does a training-free rank-capped linear transport of `W` already close the gap? `olmo-hybrid-7b` (`allenai/Olmo-Hybrid-7B`, revision `4f1cc566f9fdf3ce68da2ab6a788a83d89896dcf`; registered 2026-09-01 without an artifact receipt, C40) is the pre-registered replication substrate once its receipt exists; `olmo-3-1025-7b` (revision `a81bae42db3975be1671e27b9c9a56da1a9f980f`, registered without a receipt) remains a **throughput-matched, not architecture-matched**, descriptive reference. The dense cross-lingual EM floor gate is supplied by the registered `qwen3-0.6b-base` (a weak floor: passing proves the instrument solvable; failing at 0.6B is inconclusive).
- **Phase 1 (from scratch, 57M SWA-512 + GDN hybrids).** Does supervising `W` in half the GDN heads on public bitext, with same-prefix negatives, close ≥ 50% of the cross-lingual recall gap on surface-disjoint, beyond-window keys, at equal monolingual recall and LM loss, and beat the same loss on the S(c)-free projection pooling `P(a) = Σ_t β_t v_t k_tᵀ` (A2) by ≥ 5 EM points — *given* that the 57M/200M-token instrument is above floor (gate G1) and that `W` and `P` are numerically distinct on the spans used (gate G2)?

**Claim scope (contract sense):** architecture-causal — the contract has matched from-scratch arms and controls, which is what the validator requires for from-scratch arms. **Claim scope (what the pilot can actually establish):** the phase-1 causal contrast is *which object inside a fixed hybrid is supervised* (recurrence-weighted write vs its decay-free pooling), not GDN-vs-softmax. The operator-level contrast is registered as a conditional arm A4′ (dense + the same projection-alignment loss) and is **not claimed unless A4′ runs**. Wave 4 writes this same sentence into the contract YAML (`claim_statement`) so the contract and the prose no longer disagree (Reviewer B, Argument Coherence).

## Strategic Fit and Why Now

Three labs now ship 3-linear : 1-global hybrids (Kimi K3, Qwen3.8-Next, GLM-5.3-Flash) and none reports language- or script-controlled behaviour of the recurrent state (sweep gap seq-operators G1; benchmarks-eval G2: no translation-paired recall probes exist). The registered `Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B` is itself such a hybrid — 24 GDN layers and 8 full-attention layers at positions 3, 7, ..., 31, the same layout as Olmo Hybrid (C32) — and its `transformers` module exposes `recurrent_states` and `initial_state` (C33), so the within-model kill screen needs no unregistered checkpoint; since wave 5 it runs on the receipted base sibling `qwen3.5-4b-base` (C39). Gated DeltaNet-2 (2605.22791, 2026-05; C29) now decouples a channel-wise erase gate from a channel-wise write gate, which makes "the write" a first-class architectural object; it is the natural next substrate for this loss and does not study cross-lingual behaviour. fla `chunk_gated_delta_rule` accepts `initial_state`, returns the final state, and its backward accepts `dht`, so a loss on a state or on a state difference is differentiable without a custom kernel (C07). Kevin's assets (8xH100 with a Docker/Slurm/checkpoint harness; a receipt-bearing manifest lineage for `qwen3.5-4b` already on the host, C37; optional General Translation span-aligned corpora) make the 18-run from-scratch grid a two-day job; the pilot itself needs only public data. The fit with Kevin's "PorTAL-shaped" preference is the object: a translation-equivariance constraint on the *write* of a recurrent operator, not another harness layer.

## Mechanism and Falsifiable Predictions

**GDN recurrence (fla convention, per layer l, head h).** `S_t = S_{t−1} · α_t (I − β_t k_t k_tᵀ) + β_t v_t k_tᵀ`. Written here as `v kᵀ` (`[d_v, d_k]`); fla and the Qwen3.5/Olmo modules store the transpose `[d_k, d_v]` — convention only. In Qwen3.5-4B `k` is l2-normalized inside the kernel and the 16 key heads are repeat-interleaved to the 32 value heads (C33), so `W` is defined per value head as a `128 × 128` matrix (16,384 dims; Olmo Hybrid: `96 × 192`). The recurrence is affine in the state, so after a span `a = (a_1..a_T)` from `S_0 = S(c)`:

```text
S(c⊕a) = S(c) · ∏_{t=1..T} α_t (I − β_t k_t k_tᵀ)          (prefix carry: decay + erase acting on S(c))
       + Σ_{t=1..T} β_t v_t k_tᵀ · ∏_{s>t} α_s (I − β_s k_s k_sᵀ)   (pure write: W(a|c))
```

**Wave-3 object.** `W(a|c) := S(c⊕a) − S_{v=0}(c,a)`, where `S_{v=0}(c,a)` is computed by a second `chunk_gated_delta_rule` call over the span with `initial_state = S(c)` and `v ≡ 0` (keeps α, β, k; removes writes). By the identity above, `W` equals the state obtained by running the span from `S_0 = 0` with the same projections, so Judge B's proposed "recurrence with zero initial state" arm is algebraically the same object and is absorbed into A1 rather than run separately; the fp64 CPU doctor checks `S_{S0=0}(a) == S(c⊕a) − S_{v=0}(c,a)` to 1e-10. The wave-2 shortcut term `(∏α − 1)S(c)` is absent from `W` by construction; `W` still depends on `c` through the projections (k, v, α, β are functions of the residual stream), which is what the same-prefix negatives control.

**Wave-4 distinctness floor (gate G2, Reviewer A).** `W` differs from the control object `P(a) = Σ_t β_t v_t k_tᵀ` only through the within-span decay/erase products `∏_{s>t} α_s (I − β_s k_s k_sᵀ)`. If α is near 1 and β small over a ~30-token sentence, `W ≈ P` and the A1 − A2 contrast is a null by construction. Pre-registered: report `mean_{(l,h)∈E} cos(vec W, vec P)` on bitext spans behind shared prefixes (i) on the frozen 4B hybrid in phase 0 and (ii) on the 57M A0 hybrid at initialization and at the 60M-token selection checkpoint, since span-level α/β statistics differ between models. **Wave-5 synthetic read (C47; not a model measurement):** on random unit keys and Gaussian values at 30-token spans, `mean cos(W, P)` exceeds 0.9 whenever the per-token decay is slower than about 0.05 (α ≥ 0.95 with β ≤ 0.05; 0.9996 at α = 0.999, β = 0.05) and falls to 0.72 only at α = 0.9, β = 0.8; the doctor's own toy 2-layer GDN sits at 0.929. This says nothing about the 57M A0 or the 4B base, whose α/β statistics are unmeasured, but it makes G2 a live risk rather than a formality, so the 57M-at-initialization read is the first thing to run once the model definition exists. The A1-vs-A2 contrast is interpretable only if the E-head average is ≤ 0.9 in the model that will be trained (convention, C38). If it exceeds 0.9 the treatment/control pair is redesigned under a new versioned contract (candidates: multi-sentence spans from document-level bitext; a control `P′(a) = Σ_t v_t k_tᵀ` that also omits β); this contract's phase 1 does not run. The `W`-vs-`D` prefix-floor check from wave 3 is kept alongside.

**Treatment (A1).** `L = L_LM + λ (L_eq + L_nce)` on head subset E (half the heads of every GDN layer). `L_eq = mean_{(l,h)∈E} (1 − cos(vec W^A, vec W^B))` for a bitext pair `(a^A, a^B)` behind the same prefix `c`. `L_nce = −log exp(s(W^A, W^B)/τ) / Σ_{B'} exp(s(W^A, W^{B'})/τ)`, `s` = cosine over concatenated E-head writes, `τ = 0.07`, `λ ∈ {0.1, 0.3}` **selected per arm** (A1, A2, A3 each pick their own λ on a dev TP-MQAR-v2 built from FLORES+ dev at N = 8, subject to dev LM loss within 1% of A0; never on test — Reviewer B, believability-bar item 1). **Negatives (all behind the identical prefix `c`, so the prefix is common to positive and negatives and only content discriminates):** ≥ 2 same-prefix random language-B sentences from the same bitext document neighbourhood; ≥ 2 same-prefix hard negatives that share a named-entity string or a number with `a^A` but are not its translation; plus the batch's other-prefix spans as extras. Heads outside E carry no alignment term.

**Decisive control (A2, same-layer projection alignment, no recurrence).** Identical `L_eq + L_nce`, τ, negatives, layers and heads on `P(a) = Σ_{t∈a} β_t v_t k_tᵀ`, the decay-free, erase-free pooling of the same E heads' projections over the same span (same shape, already S(c)-free); λ selected for A2 by the same per-arm rule. A1 − A2 isolates "align the recurrence-weighted write" from "align the projections that feed it", conditional on G2.

**Placebo (A3, language-marginal).** Same loss form, own λ, with `a^B` replaced by a content-independent random language-B sentence behind the same prefix. Reading (per Judge A, wave 2): A3 tests language-ID removal on the write, which is itself a candidate mechanism; A3 ≈ A1 therefore means "the gain is language-marginal alignment, not translation equivariance", not "regularization".

**Why a span-write loss should move recall at all (Reviewer A, Argument Coherence; reasoning, not evidence).** TP-MQAR recall needs the key vector stored at the digit-code positions after `key_A sep_A` to match the query vector produced at `sep_B` after `key_B`. The loss never touches those positions directly. The bridge is the residual stream: in every GDN layer the layer output is `S_t q_t`, so the residual after the key sentence — and therefore the `k` projected at the code positions and the `q` projected at the query in *later* layers — is a function of the state the key sentence left behind. If `W(key_A|c) ≈ W(key_B|c)` in the E heads, the downstream residuals after the two keys move closer, and with them the code-position `k` and the query-position `q`. This is a chain of plausibility, not a theorem, so it is instrumented as a **pre-registered mediation diagnostic**: on the sealed test prompts, log `cos(k_code | key_A, k_code | key_B)` and `cos(q_sep | key_A, q_sep | key_B)` per layer for A0, A1 and A2; a positive A1 − A2 endpoint with no movement in these cosines would be reported as unexplained, and a failure to move recall with large cosine movement would locate the break in the readout.

**Instrument: TP-MQAR-v2.** N facts of the form "key sentence in language A, then sep_A, then a 4-digit ASCII code" among language-A distractors; query of the form "key translated into language B, then sep_B"; digit-only exact match. Key types built from FLORES+ devtest: surface-disjoint (NFKC + casefold, no shared alphanumeric token ≥ 3 chars, no shared digit string, and no shared BPE/SentencePiece subword of ≥ 4 characters under the evaluated model's tokenizer, since En↔De/Es cognates share subwords) and surface-shared (complement). N ∈ {8, 32, 128}; contexts 4K (8K for N = 128). Chance for a 4-digit code is 1e-4, so "above chance" is not a usable floor; the gates below use absolute EM.

**Pre-registered sanity checks (prefix floor and distinctness floor).** On the frozen hybrid, report `cos(W^A, W^B)` for translation pairs against the floor `cos(W^A, W^{B'})` for same-prefix non-translation spans, and the same two quantities for the old object `D`; reject the object if the translation-pair cosine of `W` is not above its same-prefix floor by ≥ 0.05 on average in ≥ 2/3 of E heads. The ledger, its 2/3-of-heads rule and the write-shrinkage counterfactual that exposes the `D` shortcut run as code in the wave-5 doctor on synthetic pairs (C45, C46): `W`'s cosines are exactly invariant to shrinking the writes because `W` is linear in `v`, while `D`'s non-translation floor rises toward 1 as the shared prefix carry takes over.

**Predictions (embarrassing if wrong).**

- P0a (descriptive, no kill): the frozen hybrid's cross-lingual gap `G(N) = EM[A=B] − EM[A≠B]` on surface-disjoint keys at N = 8 is ≥ 8 EM points larger than the dense reference's, 95% prompt-clustered bootstrap CI excluding 0 — reported with the confound list (4B post-trained hybrid vs 0.6B dense; or the Olmo pair once registered), not used as a gate.
- P0b (within-model, kill): D-zero on the fact span removes ≥ 30% of the hybrid's cross-lingual recall (relative) **and** exceeds the matched distractor-span perturbation by ≥ 10 points; **kill criterion** if the fact-minus-control difference is ≤ 10 points (state is a bystander or the drop is state/KV inconsistency).
- P0c (storage vs readout, kill): the gap exists where interference is minimal, `G(8) ≥ 8` points with monolingual EM ≥ 80%, and does not blow up with load, `G(128)/G(8)` below 2 while monolingual EM stays ≥ 60%; **kill criterion** if `G(8) ≤ 3` (states already store meaning at low load) or `G(128)/G(8) ≥ 2` (readout interference dominates; the write-format premise is unsupported and the training arm is not run).
- P0d (transport, kill, fireable): a rank-capped (r ≤ 64) per-head ridge/Procrustes map on `W`, fitted in dual form on 997 FLORES+ dev pairs x 8 prefixes, closes ≤ 40% of the hybrid's gap on devtest keys; **kill criterion** if closure ≥ 80% (meaning up to a linear language transform; training redundant). Report identity-map and random-rotation controls alongside.
- P0e (distinctness, gate G2): `mean cos(W, P)` over E heads ≤ 0.9 on the frozen hybrid and on the 57M A0 at init and 60M tokens; **redesign criterion** if the 57M value exceeds 0.9.
- P1-gate (instrument sensitivity, gate G1; Reviewers A and B): at N = 8 on surface-disjoint beyond-window keys, the best A0 LR-sweep run at 60M tokens has monolingual EM ≥ 60% and cross-lingual EM ≥ 15%, re-confirmed on A0 seed 42 at 200M tokens before the remaining 17 grid runs launch. **Rescue clause:** if A0 cross-lingual EM is below 15% but the dense A4 seed-42 run reaches ≥ 25% at the same checkpoint, the instrument is solvable at this scale, the hybrid's floor is itself a reportable finding, and phase 1 proceeds under the floor-compression rule below. **Defer criterion** (not a kill): neither condition holds — phase 1 is deferred and bitext share, recall curriculum or model size are revised under a new versioned contract with a new output path (hard rule 3).
- P1 (phase 1, 3 seeds, beyond-window surface-disjoint keys): A1 closes ≥ 50% of A0's gap with monolingual EM within 2 points and held-out LM loss within 0.5%; A2 closes ≤ 25%; A3 ≤ 10%; seed-mean A1 − A2 ≥ 5 EM points with all 3 seed pairs positive. **Decision rule (wave 5, simulation-derived, C48):** promote if the seed mean of A1 − A2 is ≥ 5 EM points, all three seed pairs are positive and the pooled prompt-clustered 95% CI excludes 0; **falsifier / kill** if any seed pair is negative or the seed mean is negative, or A3 closes ≥ 50% of A1's closure, or LM loss > 1% worse, or `mean ||W||` in E heads shrinks > 30%, or A5 (no bitext) ≈ A0 within 2 points while A1 beats only A5; any other outcome is an underpowered band (simulated probability 0.37 at a true 5-point effect) sent to the +2-seed line under a new contract, not a kill. The wave-4 rule (every pair ≥ 5) passed a true 5-point effect only 12.5% of the time (C48) and is retired, not erased. **Floor-compression rule (Reviewer B):** if A1 and A2 both sit below 10% cross-lingual EM in every seed, the primary endpoint is declared floor-compressed and no inference is drawn in either direction; if A0 cross-lingual EM ≥ 90% at N = 8 (no gap to close) the N = 32 cells become primary by pre-registration.
- P2 (held-out functional criteria, not optimized by the loss): (i) closure on surface-disjoint keys within 10 points of closure on surface-shared keys; (ii) `G(N)` for A1 at N ∈ {8, 32, 128} shrinks at every N (not only at N = 8); (iii) swap `S ← S_{v=0}(c,a^A) + W^B` on devtest keys behind never-trained prefixes preserves ≥ 80% recall in A1 vs ≤ 40% in A0; (iv) mediation cosines (above) move in A1 relative to A0 and A2. The swap is secondary because it is monotone in the trained cosine; the primary endpoint is recall EM, which the loss never sees.

**Strongest counter-argument (devil's advocate).** The cross-lingual gap in GDN states may be entirely a readout phenomenon — the state stores the same content as the KV cache, and a translated query simply has a smaller `qᵀS` margin over N−1 distractors, rescued in the dense model by softmax's winner-take-all — in which case aligning writes cannot help and only a readout-side change (or QED-style query-derived erase) can. P0c is the pre-registered test of exactly this account, and its "readout dominates" branch kills the training arm before any from-scratch GPU is spent. A second counter-argument, added in wave 4: at 57M parameters and 200M tokens no arm may recall translated FLORES+ sentences at all, in which case the grid measures nothing; G1 exists to convert that outcome into a cheap, explicit deferral instead of a false negative. **What's missing:** a direct margin measurement is descriptive only (we log the correct-value logit minus best-distractor logit at the answer position, by N and query language); we have no theory predicting the *size* of a storage-format gap, so the thresholds 8 and 3 points, and the wave-4 gate thresholds 60/15/25/0.9/10 (C38), are conventions, not derived; and 57M SWA-512 hybrids may not reproduce the 4B or 7B regime even if both phases pass.

## Evaluation, Statistics, and Leakage Checks

Protocols followed: `.claude/skills/experimental-design/SKILL.md`, `.claude/skills/statistical-power/SKILL.md`, and `ext/ars/academic-paper-reviewer/references/statistical_reporting_standards.md`.

**Primary endpoint (units fixed in wave 4).** The A1 − A2 difference in cross-lingual EM, in EM points, on surface-disjoint TP-MQAR-v2 keys at beyond-window positions, N = 8 primary (N = 32 becomes primary only under the pre-registered ceiling rule). Unit of analysis = the seed-paired arm difference (three pairs: A1 and A2 share initialization and data order within a seed). Within each seed pair, a prompt-clustered bootstrap (clusters = FLORES+ key sentence) gives a 95% CI for that pair's difference. Promotion rule (wave 5, C48): the seed mean of the three differences ≥ 5 EM points, all three pairs positive, and the pooled prompt-clustered 95% CI excludes 0; kill on any negative pair or a negative seed mean; anything else is an underpowered band sent to the +2-seed line. The closure fraction relative to A0's gap is a secondary, derived quantity. Report effect sizes (EM-point differences with CIs and Cohen's d on seed-paired differences), seed SD, and the resolution ratio per comparison.

**Minimum worthwhile effect.** 5 EM points for A1 − A2 (below this the write object is not worth its 1.5x training cost over projection alignment); 50% gap closure for A1 vs A0.

**Noise estimate and power (C28).** The seed-level SD of a cross-lingual EM gap at 57M is unknown; the nearest published floor is a loss-asymptote spread ≤ 0.02 across 3 seeds at ~300M (C24), which does not convert to EM. Assumed seed-paired difference SD = 3 EM points (assumption, pre-registered check). Closed form (paired t, two-sided α = 0.05, scipy noncentral t): 3 seeds give power 0.37 at δ = 5 and 0.66 at δ = 8; 5 seeds give 0.79 and 0.99. Three seeds are therefore a **kill screen**, not a confirmation: a seed-mean A1 − A2 ≥ 5 with 3/3 pairs positive and the pooled CI excluding 0 promotes to the +2-seed line; a single opposite-signed seed kills (wave-5 rule below). Prompt level (C23 formula with ω = 0, K = 1): 785 paired prompts detect 5 points at 80% power for a paired-difference SD of 0.5 (1,538 at 0.7); each seed evaluates 1,800 surface-disjoint cross-cell prompts (6 cells x 3 N x 100), which meets the 0.5 case and nearly the 0.7 case.

**Wave-5 re-derivation of the decision rule (Reviewer B, C48).** The wave-4 rule required every seed pair to clear 5 points; under the same SD-3 assumption the doctor's 200,000-draw simulation (closed form Φ((δ−5)/3)³) passes a true 5-point effect with probability 0.125 and an 8-point effect with 0.594, so the rule killed the effect it declared worth detecting 87.5% of the time and the paired-t power figures above did not describe the decision actually taken. The registered rule is now: promote on seed-mean A1 − A2 ≥ 5 with all three pairs positive and the pooled prompt-clustered 95% CI excluding 0 (half-width ≈ 1.9 EM points at 1,800 prompts per seed with a per-prompt paired SD of 0.7, so the CI clause is non-binding once the mean clears 5 and exists to catch a degenerate prompt set); kill only on any negative pair or a negative seed mean; every other outcome is an underpowered band sent to the +2-seed line. Simulated operating characteristics at δ = 0 / 3 / 5 / 8 / 10 EM points: promote 0.002 / 0.121 / 0.490 / 0.952 / 0.997; kill 0.875 / 0.404 / 0.137 / 0.012 / 0.001; underpowered 0.123 / 0.475 / 0.373 / 0.037 / 0.002. Three seeds remain a kill screen; the noise model is still an assumption to be checked against A0's measured seed SD, and the rule is mirrored in the contract's `small-model-identifiability` promotion gate and falsifiers (contract_version 5).

**Floor and ceiling handling (extended to the primary endpoint in wave 4).** Gate G1 must pass before the grid launches. If A1 and A2 both sit below 10% cross-lingual EM in every seed, the primary endpoint is floor-compressed: no inference either way, outcome "instrument at floor at 57M/200M". If A0 cross-lingual EM ≥ 90% at N = 8, the N = 32 cells become primary. Phase-0 ratio statistics (P0c) exclude any cell with EM near 0 or 100, as in wave 3.

**Randomization and blocking.** Seeds {42, 43, 44} are shared across arms: A0/A1/A2/A3/A5 share initialization and data order per seed (A4 has its own init per seed, same data order); the seed-42 runs of A0, A1, A2 and A3 are resumed from their 60M selection checkpoints (same schedule, same data order; equivalence attested by the resume test), seeds 43 and 44 run from scratch; arm-by-seed jobs are scheduled on the 8 GPUs in a Latin-square order so no arm is confounded with a GPU or a time slot, except that A0 seed 42 and A4 seed 42 run first for the G1 reads; evaluation prompt sets and fact positions are fixed per seed by a recorded permutation; λ (per arm) and LR (A0) are chosen on FLORES+ dev only.

**Multiplicity.** One primary endpoint; the five secondaries (P2 i–iv, A5 vs A0) are Holm-corrected; phase-0 kills and the G1/G2 gates are pre-registered thresholds, not tests.

**Assumption checks.** Paired differences inspected for sign consistency (3 seeds cannot support normality tests); prompt-level CIs use clustered bootstrap; EM near 0 or 100 in any cell is flagged as floor/ceiling compression per the rule above.

**Leakage.** FLORES+ dedup against training (exact + MinHash 5-gram); values are per-prompt random digit codes never present in training; retrieval-impossible and value-permutation controls at chance; keys never appear in the synthetic curriculum; transport maps fitted on dev, evaluated on devtest; λ and LR selected on the dev TP-MQAR-v2 (FLORES+ dev sentences), never on devtest; test prompts sealed with hashes before any phase-1 run; G1 reads use the sealed test prompts once per checkpoint and are logged, so the number of test reads before the grid (smoke, 4 sweep checkpoints, A0 and A4 seed 42 at 60M and 200M) is fixed and disclosed.

## Safety, Data Rights, and Monitorability

**Monitorability.** Chain-of-thought is untouched and there is no action channel; the intervention is a pretraining auxiliary loss on recurrent-state writes plus read-only state interventions on frozen models. A meaning-level state could help a monitor trained in one language transfer to others, or could remove surface cues monitors rely on; both directions are reported if the direction proceeds, against the 13-language fragility set (C25).

**Data rights.** ParaCrawl CC0; FLORES+ CC BY-SA 4.0 with gated acceptance of terms (the derived TP-MQAR-v2 is released share-alike); SCB-MT-EN-TH-2020 CC BY-SA 4.0; FineWeb/FineWeb-2 ODC-By 1.0 with attribution (FineWeb's license is from card memory and must be re-verified before release); Olmo checkpoints Apache-2.0; `Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B-Base`, `Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B` and `Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B-Base` Apache-2.0 per the registry. **General Translation parallel data (C44): the 2026-09-01 inventory excludes customer translation memory from every pilot under ToS §3.1 (no research processing purpose is recorded; a RoPA entry, DPIA and customer notice would be needed and this repository cannot make that decision); only GT-owned 8-locale docs pairs are usable, as a small evaluation set; the GT arm stays optional and customer content is never used.** IP: the contribution is a loss on top of fla kernels with no kernel-level delta-rule change, so the NVIDIA "Gated delta networks" patent flag (US20260105282A1, pending) does not attach; state interventions are inference-time reads/writes of a cache, not a kernel; Gated DeltaNet-2 (NVlabs) is cited as prior art and not modified.

**Red lines.** Stop if any released artifact contains FLORES+ or bitext text beyond CC BY-SA/CC0 terms; stop if the derived instrument leaks sealed test prompts (the G1 reads are counted and disclosed; any read beyond the pre-registered list is a leak); no customer data; no model is trained on any language's text whose source license forbids it.

## Negative-Result Value

Phase 0 alone produces the first within-model, state-intervention measurement of cross-lingual recall in a 2026 GDN hybrid, on a registered checkpoint with a receipt lineage: "state is a bystander" (P0b), "readout interference, not storage" (P0c), or "meaning up to a rank-64 linear transform" (P0d) each closes sweep gap G2 cheaply and is publishable, and the `W`-vs-`D` and `W`-vs-`P` tables document, respectively, a shortcut every future recurrent-state alignment loss must avoid and whether "the write" is even a distinct object from its projections on sentence-length spans. Phase-1 negatives: G1 deferral says a 57M/200M hybrid cannot do translated recall on FLORES+ keys at all (an instrument-scaling fact, reported as such); A1 ≈ A2 says the recurrence-weighted write adds nothing over projection alignment; A3 ≈ A1 says the gain is language-ID removal; closure only on surface-shared keys documents a copy shortcut; A5 ≈ A0 with A1 > A5 only says the effect is bitext exposure. TP-MQAR-v2, the intervention toolkit and the selection ledger remain the G2/G20 instrument in every branch.

**What's missing** (devil's advocate, continued): a positive phase 1 at 57M with SWA-512 does not transfer to 4B or 7B full-attention-every-4th-layer hybrids where softmax heads can carry recall; the proposal claims only the 57M regime and states the transfer question as future work with its own contract. The G1 and G2 thresholds are reviewer-proposed conventions (C38), so a near-threshold outcome (for example cross-lingual EM of 14% or a `cos(W, P)` of 0.91) will be reported as borderline rather than treated as a clean pass or fail.
