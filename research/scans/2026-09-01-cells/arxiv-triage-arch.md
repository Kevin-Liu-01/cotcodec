## Kill-shot assessment against current directions

No paper in scope is a clean kill-shot. Verdicts with the closest pressure:

- **Dir 16 (portable sidecar update dynamics).** Not killed. MoNe (2608.17616) is the closest occupant: a fast-weight memory sidecar attached to a frozen pretrained Transformer without retraining. It makes no held-out task-base transfer claim and is a long-context memory, not a task-conditioned episode-scoped update rule. Fast Weight Attention (2608.27763) systematizes the update-rule family (Falcon-1/2/3 and -A), so dir 16 cannot claim rule-family novelty and must draw its rule from, or baseline against, that taxonomy. E2-TTT (2608.21308) is a from-scratch layer, not a sidecar.
