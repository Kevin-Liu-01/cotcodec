## Occupancy shifts since 2026-08-10

1. **Latent reasoning × tool use → occupied (first-party).** The 2026-08-10 table listed "tool use, monitorability, and causal tests against shortcuts" as the latent-reasoning gap. Looped LMs on API-Bank/BFCL/NESTful (2608.18171) fill the tool-use slot under matched SFT, with depth-saturation at 3 iterations and adaptive depth winning on cost. Remaining gap: causal tests against shortcuts and monitorability of *what* the loop computes.
2. **Diffusion-native tool interaction → concept staked, empirics open.** CID (2608.10438) claims the architecture of observation-driven re-denoising; DCGC (2608.25428), SRD (2608.22090) and DiffPDE (2608.30532) occupy draft/trace/code repair. The "use case where repeated passes beat AR at equal wall time" now has a theory of serial depth (2608.25505) and a real-hardware ledger (2608.23807) but still no positive equal-wall-time result on agent tasks.
3. **Portable adaptation: frozen artifact + thin alignment is doubly occupied.** Engram reader transfer (2608.17050: cross-model 38.5 = same-model 38.5 vs no-memory 32.1 on Mistral-7B) and MentorPulse (2608.20927: 52.2% of mentor–student gap, beats equal-budget LoRA) leave only *update-rule* portability to dir 16.
4. **Context→LoRA hypernetworks now include budgeted rank allocation** (Omni2LoRA, 2608.09227: GRPO policy, 30% rank budget, 12× TTFT), adjacent to rank-adaptive summaries.
5. **Adaptive depth: gated recurrent modulation supersedes MoR as the baseline** (2608.15062, 9/9 cells), and two theory papers give operator-level cost/safety criteria (2608.18222, 2608.26556). Small-model ICL + recurrent latent reasoning is claimed by BDH-CQ (2608.09888, 29.5% pass@2 ARC-AGI-1 at 150M).
6. **CoT monitorability is filling fast:** tool-return channel unfaithfulness (FACE-Eval, 15 models), hidden-directive disclosure asymmetry with a shared hiding direction (ICG), hidden-CoT detection score (HCDS), decorative medical CoT (CDR 72.9%), capability–auditability tradeoff (Why2Speak), dense intent channel (Intent-as-a-Tool), and mid-layer legible latent chains in an omni model (2608.24958). The **cross-lingual monitorability sub-axis is nearly empty** (one n = 8 case study, 2608.15392) — a fit for the team's translation assets and the "refusal consistency across languages" line of the eval contract.
7. **Agent memory growth has a named pathology and an oracle-world construction** (catastrophic remembering, 2608.11095: 1,867 repos, +226% growth, inverted IFEval with known optimal prompts). Dir 17 should reuse the verifiable-world idea for its paired CPU oracle and add rationale-comment deletion as a non-randomized control.
8. **dLLM inference cost dropped by an order of magnitude on paper** (CAI-DLLM 18–45×, structured suffix up to 72.81× combined, survival length control 7×, CARVE ≈0.5× FLOPs) — any matched-wall-time comparison against AR in the diffusion repair cell must use these as the dLLM baseline stack, not vanilla LLaDA decoding.
