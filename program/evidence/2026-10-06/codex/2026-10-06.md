## Findings that change the next comparison

### 1. Recurrent-memory use already has a direct intervention baseline

**Signal 4: update closest-prior comparison.** Lee et al.,
[Balancing Memory Pathways](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2610.06750),
October 5, 2026, inspect Qwen3.5-4B and Nemotron-H-4B-Instruct.
Their intervention masks attention across the context/query boundary while
preserving recurrent propagation. The complementary condition resets recurrent
carry at the boundary while preserving attention; it does not remove all
attention or recurrent layers.

They add a recurrent-path auxiliary objective alongside SFT. Table 1 reports
Qwen QA micro-average accuracy of 34.6% for SFT and 38.5% with the auxiliary
objective. They halve optimization steps to compensate for the extra pass.
These are the authors' results, not our measurements. See sections 3–5 and
Table 1.

**Implication for D22:** neither “a hybrid underuses recurrence” nor “restrict
attention to train memory” is a defensible novelty claim. Our remaining question
is whether translation-aligned *pure state writes* add value beyond this
pathway-use intervention and projection alignment. That distinction still
needs a closest-prior/code audit. It is not proven novel by this scan.

**Boundary:** their Qwen3.5-4B experiments do not establish behavior of our pinned
Qwen3.5-4B-Base artifact. Training-data, checkpoint, token and wall-time matching
must be checked before treating the reported compute matching as our own.

### 2. Language control can also change the retrieval action

**Signal 4: strengthen intervention checks.** Hauck et al.,
[Reasoning-Language Alignment in Monolingual RAG](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2610.03136),
October 2, 2026, vary reasoning language in a German agentic RAG task with
Qwen3-30B-A3B. They use 585 single-passage and 30 multi-hop questions.

On the single-passage set, forced German scores 3.701, French 3.491 and
unconstrained reasoning 3.718 on a five-point judge scale. German versus French
is significant after Holm correction; German does not significantly outperform
unconstrained reasoning. The paper also reports qualitative cases in which
French reasoning produces French retrieval queries against the German corpus.
See sections 4–6 and Tables 2–4.

**Implication for Paper 1:** retain an unconstrained baseline and measure the
language and semantic fidelity of tool arguments. A schema remaining English
does not prove that query text stayed fixed. Report leakage separately from
token savings, and separate one-hop from multi-hop tasks.

**Boundary:** one model family, one evidence language, a small multi-hop set,
and imperfect judging. The no-reasoning advantage is partly consistent with
answer-length bias. This does not establish an optimal reasoning language or
prove a token-saving routing policy.

### 3. Memory comparisons need token budgets and retrieval-failure breakdowns

**Signal 4: add a matched simple control.** Sharma and Lall,
[When Does Selection Replace Extraction?](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2609.34227),
September 28, 2026, compare raw turns selected by Jev with their own extraction
system. The primary LoCoMo test uses 778 questions from five held-out
conversations. Raw-turn selection is non-inferior within the registered
five-point margin, not equivalent or superior: 77.0% versus 77.5%, at mean
contexts of 265 versus 251 tokens.

The generous-budget comparison is descriptive and not token-matched.
At k=20 the selected raw-turn path often cannot fill its budget because of its
threshold and fallback rules. The extraction comparator was not run on full
LongMemEval. See sections 4, 5.1, 5.3 and 5.5.

**Implication for memory policy:** compare raw history plus a strong selector
before attributing gains to extraction, graphs or consolidation. Record actual
tokens, candidate recall, selection loss, abstention, write cost and read cost.

**Boundary:** five conversation clusters, one author-created comparator,
model-based selection and judging, and provider/serving caveats. Do not
generalize a five-point non-inferiority result into “extraction is unnecessary.”
No Jev account use or paid calls were made here.

### 4. Component-level memory correctness is separate from final QA

**Signal 4: benchmark-adapter review candidate.**
[DyadMem](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2610.03020), first indexed October 2,
2026, separates capture, update, recall, gold-memory QA and full-pipeline QA.
Standalone update is gold-conditioned; the full pipeline uses the model's own
bank. It can therefore expose failures that a final answer score obscures.
See sections 3–4.

**Implication:** inspect its component contracts as a possible additional
evaluation surface. Do not replace our durability, restart, tenancy or purge
doctors. Its unsafe deletion metric concerns deleting the wrong logical
memory entry, not proving bytes were erased from disk or backups.

**Checks before adoption:** generated-dialogue provenance, split construction,
semantic matcher and judge behavior, treatment of no-change sessions, and code
agreement with the paper. The main update score excludes no-change sessions.
Section 3.3 also appears to invert the prose definition of correct/incorrect
QA credit, while the reported results use accuracy-style numbers; verify the
released scorer before implementing an adapter.

The gold-to-pipeline gap is an end-to-end difference, not causal attribution to
individual stages. A benchmark description is not adapter admission.

### 5. First-eligible counterfactual branching is not our novelty

**Signal 4: extend D17's collision ledger.** Li et al.,
[CIPO](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2610.04991), October 4, 2026, compare atomic
tool actions with executable composite skills. They branch at the first eligible
granularity decision, continue under the same policy, and use the paired outcome
difference as an auxiliary reward. Their four-rollout budget includes up to two
base/branch pairs. See section 3.4.

**Implication for D17:** first-eligible branching, paired continuations and
outcome-driven control are already present in adjacent work. The proposed
distinction remains prospective, known-propensity assignment of one retained
memory item's first service, with write-time-only covariates and an explicit
population estimand. It still needs code-level and statistical discrimination.

**Boundary:** their intervention changes action granularity, not retained-item
service. Their task-success metric counts both partial and yes labels from an
LLM judge. Five judge evaluations of the same 100 episodes are not five
independent sets of trajectories. Do not compare those percentages with
strict executable outcome scores.
