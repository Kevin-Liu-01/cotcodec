Context compressors that sit outside the target model, such as small token classifiers or sentence selectors trained to keep the informative part of a context, are audited across ten languages in five writing systems on fully parallel items: a reading-comprehension benchmark with a passage and a question in the same language, longer parallel documents, and a long-context arm that embeds the target passage among same-language distractors. Each compressed context is sent with its question to target models through their interfaces at requested keep rates of 0.75, 0.5 and 0.33, beside deterministic baselines matched to the compressor's achieved token budget in the target's tokenizer (truncation, random word deletion, sentence extraction by term weighting). Accuracy is normalized between a no-context and a full-context anchor into the fraction of usable context value retained, and the transfer gap is that fraction in English minus that in each other language, with paired bootstrap intervals over identical items. Compressors trained with English supervision are contrasted with one trained on many languages, to separate the effect of the supervision language from that of the architecture.
