A multilingual cross-encoder reranker, which reads a query and a retrieved passage together, is given an extra per-token head that marks each passage token as relevant or not to the query; a sentence is kept when most of its tokens are marked, and the sentences not kept are removed before the passage reaches a separate generator model. The pruning head is trained by per-token classification on query-passage pairs whose labels come from an English dataset, used as is, translated sentence by sentence into sixteen languages, or re-annotated in those languages, while the reranker's encoder covers about a hundred languages. Pruned and unpruned contexts are compared on the generator's answer quality and on the share of context removed across several multilingual question-answering benchmarks. On one benchmark the query and the context are put in the same language, with languages seen and unseen in the pruning head's training reported separately, and in a second setting the context is in English while the query is in another language.
