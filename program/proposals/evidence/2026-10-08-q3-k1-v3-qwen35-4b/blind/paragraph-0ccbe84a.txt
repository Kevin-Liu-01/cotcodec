A frozen pretrained decoder language model whose layers alternate between a fixed-size recurrent state and, at every fourth layer, softmax attention is fitted at each softmax-attention layer with a small learned selector that scores four-token blocks of the context from a few query projections of the current token and one pooled key per block. Each selector is trained only by minimizing the KL divergence between its block distribution and a block aggregation of its own layer's attention (head-summed mass or within-block maximum); the backbone receives no gradient, and the selectors' training text covers some writing systems and not others. At a budget of 1,024 of 8,192 context tokens, a selector's recall of a known evidence passage is compared with the recall of the exact top blocks of its own training target, as a difference-in-differences: how much more recall the selector loses than its target when the question is a human translation in a different writing system from the passage than when it is in the passage's language. Averaging the two translation directions isolates the mismatch between question and passage scripts; half their difference isolates a deficit tied to the question's script, and a deficit tied to the passage's script cancels. Evidence counts only passage blocks sharing no content token with either question, for questions without named-entity or number anchors. Because literal matches can still act on the same-language question, through competition for the budget and through the blocks that follow a match, the contrast is recomputed on two literal-free evidence sets: one that removes from both evidence and candidates every block within two blocks of an exact or near-literal match, and one restricted to passage text before the first match, which a causal model cannot contextualize with it; a selector that scores matches and the blocks after them is the positive control. A negative result needs the cross-script excess, its question-script part, the excess for writing systems seen in training, both literal-free versions and every layer with enough headroom to lie inside a two-sided band, with seed variance estimated layer by layer from independently initialized selectors; a positive result needs the excess to survive on the literal-free evidence. Gates require the dense model to show headroom on the masked passage and to answer better with it than without it before any selector is trained.
