A small selector is attached to every full-attention layer of a frozen pretrained transformer, including hybrid models that interleave linear and full attention. It scores blocks of queries against blocks of keys with a few low-dimensional projections, and it is trained only by KL distillation to the frozen layer's attention distribution, either over all keys or only over its own top-K selected set, on about 100 million tokens of English web text at a 16K context. At inference it replaces dense attention with block-sparse attention over its top-K key blocks, at a static budget of one half or three quarters of the context or at a cumulative-probability threshold. Accuracy on English long-context retrieval and reasoning benchmarks, perplexity and end-to-end throughput are compared with the dense model.
