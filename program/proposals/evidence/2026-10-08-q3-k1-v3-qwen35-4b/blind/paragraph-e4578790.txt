A frozen pretrained decoder language model whose layers alternate between a fixed-size recurrent state and, at every fourth layer, softmax attention is fitted at each softmax-attention layer with a small learned selector that scores four-token blocks of the context from a few query projections of the current token and one pooled key per block. Each selector is trained only by minimizing the KL divergence between its block distribution and a block aggregation of its own layer's attention (head-summed mass or within-block maximum); the backbone receives no gradient, and the selectors' training text covers some writing systems and not others. At a budget of 1,024 of 8,192 context tokens, a selector's recall of a known evidence passage is compared with the recall of the exact top blocks of its own training target, as a difference-in-differences: how much more recall the selector loses than its target when the question is a human translation in a different writing system from the passage than when it is in the passage's language. The two translation directions are read separately, because a deficit tied only to the question's script, or only to the passage's script, enters them with opposite signs. Evidence counts only passage blocks that share no content token with either question, for questions without named-entity or number anchors, and the contrast is recomputed on two literal-free evidence sets: the blocks more than two blocks away from any exact or near-literal match, and the passage text before its first match, pooled over every passage that has some, which a causal model cannot contextualize with a later match. A positive result needs the excess to be large on the masked evidence, present in both translation directions, present on both literal-free sets, and present as a loss of retained headroom on a logarithmic as well as a linear scale, while the selector keeps at least half of its target's same-language headroom in each direction; independent question-side and passage-side deficits, or a selector collapsing to its floor, therefore cannot produce it. A negative result needs the excess, both directions, the excess for writing systems seen in training, both literal-free versions and every layer with enough headroom to lie inside a two-sided band, with seed variance estimated layer by layer from independently initialized selectors. Gates require, before any selector is trained, that the dense model show headroom on the masked passage, answer better with the passage than without it, and that the literal-free evidence retain at least half of an injected cross-script degradation of the dense target.
