An open reasoning language model is run in its thinking mode, producing a long internal reasoning segment before a final boxed answer, and six answers are sampled independently for each mathematics problem at the model's recommended settings. Problems are grouped into families by shared provenance (the same contest instance or source competition) and by near-duplicate links, and whole families are assigned half to training and half to held-out. For every sampled answer, one teacher-forced pass over the stored tokens records the residual-stream activation at every layer at the position that predicts the first token of the final answer, after the reasoning segment has ended. A standardized, L2-regularized logistic classifier of answer correctness is fitted on training families only, with the layer and the regularization strength chosen by family-grouped cross-validated log loss. On held-out families four quantities are read, each with a 90% family-cluster bootstrap interval and a three-way decision: the mean over mixed-correctness problems of the within-problem ranking AUC (line 0.60); its paired increment over a classifier fitted the same way on output-only features (trace lengths, token log-probabilities and entropy, and the answer's vote share among the six draws), which must be positive; the share of problems whose six draws are contested, two to four correct, an identified functional of the per-problem pass-rate distribution used because the share of problems whose true pass rate lies in a middle band is not identified from six draws (line 0.10); and the accuracy of a classifier-weighted vote minus plain majority voting, which must not be negative. A small pilot on training families first measures trace lengths, disagreement rates and accelerator throughput and fixes, by a registered rule, which difficulty strata and how many problems fit a fixed compute cap; generation and the separate activation pass are charged in measured whole-job accelerator time. Passing all four lines only licenses a later factorial study of answer selection crossed with per-problem re-sampling.
