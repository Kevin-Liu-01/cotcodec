A frozen pretrained decoder language model is shown episodes of synthetic tasks: eight input-output demonstrations followed by queries, and its next-token distribution at each query's answer position is recorded. The same frozen model then reads each query without the demonstrations while a small state is applied at four residual-stream depths: the residual is projected onto a fixed 64-dimensional subspace estimated from generic web text without any task text, multiplied by a low-rank matrix and written back into the subspace. States are fitted per episode to minimize the KL divergence to the model's own eight-shot predictions on 24 fitting queries and are scored on 8 held-out queries. The classes are nested and matched in rank: one constant state per task family (fitted on all its episodes); plus a free per-episode shift that ignores the query (a label prior); plus a rank-8 per-episode term acting on the centred query representation, whose read directions are either confined to the span of the eight demonstrations' own representations at that depth (encoded alone and encoded inside the eight-shot prompt, after one linear map shared by all episodes of the family and fitted on their fitting queries) or unconstrained. The confined class contains every write built from outer products with mapped demonstration representations, including first-order, preconditioned and recursive-least-squares updates and linear-attention memories. Another episode's fitted query-dependent term, placed on top of the episode's own shift, serves as a placebo: a family counts as episode-specific only if each episode's own state beats it, and only if the per-episode shift does not explain the model's sensitivity to deranged labels. The decision quantities are the share of the remaining held-out divergence the unconstrained term removes and the shortfall of the confined class. Before anything is read, the family constant must leave no residual that the average per-episode term removes, planted states of known class must be recovered and told apart, planted writers that use preconditioned or differently contextualised demonstration keys must not read as gaps, and a planted per-episode shift must read as episode-constant. The gate stops if too few families show label-dependent learning, if their behaviour is one state per family plus a label prior, if the unconstrained term removes less than half of the remaining divergence, or if the confined class ties; a gap licenses only a later study and is pre-registered as not identifying a non-gradient learning rule.
