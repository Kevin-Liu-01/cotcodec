A small hybrid language model, in which nine linear-recurrent layers with a gated delta-rule matrix state are interleaved with three sliding-window attention layers of window 512, is trained from scratch with next-token prediction only on one billion tokens of four languages: three quarters monolingual text, one fifth translation pairs (half presented as two sequences that share a bitwise-identical preceding context, half side by side in one sequence), and one twentieth a same-language curriculum of facts (a natural sentence followed by a four-digit code) that are queried later in the sequence. The model is then probed with prompts that state eight such facts and later ask for one fact's code by giving its sentence, either repeated verbatim or translated into a language with another script, sharing no word, four-letter string or digit with any stated sentence. In the main condition the whole block of facts lies farther back than the largest distance the attention layers and short convolutions can bridge without the recurrent state (1,560 positions), so a correct answer must have been carried by the state. This is verified on each trained checkpoint: with the recurrent update replaced by its zero-state form at every position after the facts, the output at the answer must stay exactly unchanged when the facts are replaced. Exact match on the code is judged against the one-in-eight rate of answers that copy any stated code, through a binding margin (target hits minus distractor hits). The seven distractor sentences are the target's neighbours in an ordering by punctuation and length, and each translated query is paired with a decoy, an unrelated sentence with the same punctuation, length and name pattern, whose binding margin is subtracted, so that only matching beyond surface form counts. On the same checkpoint, with the facts inside one attention window, attention across the end of the facts block is blocked or the recurrent state after it is removed, so that attention-only and state-only cross-lingual matching are compared at the same distance. Each of three seeds is classified against fixed lines (in-language recall at least 60%, cross-script recall at least 15%, both margins above zero) with intervals clustered by sentence. The step trains no new objective and claims no new method: it is the precondition for a later study of an auxiliary objective that makes the recurrent-state write for a sentence match the write for its translation.
