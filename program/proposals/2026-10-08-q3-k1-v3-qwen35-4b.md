## Claim and Research Question

The experiment under review is the draft registration
`program/preregistrations/q3-k1-localization-screen-v3.md` (new experiment
id `q3-k1-localization-screen-v3`, status DRAFT). It is the K1 v3 that program
decision D26 called for after K1 v2's honest gauntlet exit (score 45, triad 3
of 3 refuted). Its base is the one the dense pre-check v2 licensed:
Qwen3.5-4B-Base at revision `1001bb4d`, combined read NEGATIVE_CAPABLE_V3
(E1). This proposal argues for that design and states its defects. The
registration carries all seven requirements of the combined read and answers
the independent verifier's three caveats; the traceability table at the end
of the registration maps each one to a clause.

Registered question (v3, "Question"): on frozen Qwen3.5-4B-Base, do
block-form (compress ratio 4) indexers fitted on its eight softmax-attention
layers, each distilled by KL from that layer's own attention, lose more
needle-selection recall than the dense block top-k of their own distillation
target when the question is a human translation in a different script from
the needle passage, compared with a question in the needle's own language?
Recall counts only needle blocks that share no content token with either
question (overlap-masked recall), on entity-unanchored questions, at 1,024 of
8,192 tokens. The primary stratum is the seven scripts indexer training never
saw. The comparator stratum is three scripts it did see (th, hi, km).

Variable and statistic. For target T in {hs (head-sum), mp (QSA max-pool)},
xi^M_T is the macro over the 14 unseen-stratum pairs of the controlled-family
mean of [R^M_ind(MN) − R^M_ind(CX)] − [R^M_T(MN) − R^M_T(CX)] in recall
points. R^M is masked recall, R_ind the seed-averaged indexer at the frozen
learning rate, R_T the dense top-256 blocks of its own target, MN the question
in the needle's language and CX the human-translated question in the other
language. The co-statistic is xi_rel^M_T, the macro of G^M(MN) − G^M(CX) with
G^M(c) = (R^M_ind(c) − R^M_rand(c)) / (R^M_T(c) − R^M_rand(c)).

Claim scope: **attachment-capability.** The indexers are trained KL-only on a
frozen backbone. That is the Stage-1 state that QSA reports degrades quality
until joint sparse training (C04), and production indexers are co-trained
(C05, C14). No outcome is a statement about production DSA or QSA indexers,
and none is an architecture claim (rule 8).

What this proposal claims:

1. The screen measures one quantity that no source in the stated coverage
   reports for any model: the excess evidence-recall loss of a KL-distilled
   in-model selector over its own dense target under a cross-script question.
2. On Qwen3.5-4B-Base the registered design can reach both decisive verdicts.
   GO and NEGATIVE are identified against the three confounds that sank K1 v2
   (literal overlap, a flat selector, script exposure), to the extent stated
   below.
3. The cheapest decisive steps run first: zero-GPU bundle facts, a dense-only
   development pre-step, and a dense-only H2 gate on the audit partition.
   Indexers are trained only after those pass.

What it does not claim: that a decisive verdict is likely. The synthesis
owner's judgement (Mechanism section) is that the screen reaches GO or
NEGATIVE with probability about 0.2 to 0.5, and that its likeliest single stop
is the H2 gate. It also does not claim that overlap masking removes every
lexical channel. It removes shared tokens from the measured evidence, but
literal matches elsewhere in the context still compete for the budget, which
the two-sided literal check bounds rather than removes.

Decision this proposal serves: admission. The synthesis projection of the
registered caps is 7.21 GPU-h central and 9.79 high (E7). Under D24 any total
over 8 GPU-h waits on Kevin, and D26 already reserves the K1 v3 decision for
him. Kevin can admit v3 as drafted, admit it with an owner option (decision 64:
2,000 primary-partition cells for dense-only H2), cut descriptive legs, or
stop Q3.

## Strategic Fit and Why Now

- The dense pre-check v2 changed what K1 can answer (E1). On Qwen3-0.6B-Base
  a NEGATIVE was out of reach (H1_CX 12.25 points). On Qwen3.5-4B-Base the
  dense target's cross-script selection headroom is 42.15 points (99 percent
  interval 35.76 to 48.20), and its own same-language advantage is only 2.23
  points. So a selector's excess loss has room to show, and so does its
  absence.
- The base is now the exact setting of the most direct apparatus prior.
  SpotAttention fits a KL-distilled lightning-indexer-style selector to every
  full-attention layer of frozen Qwen3.5-4B. It trains on English FineWeb-Edu
  only, and its Limitations say non-English data is untested (C02).
  Oracle-Guided Sparse Prefill distils frozen KL indexers on Qwen3.5 hybrid
  full-attention layers. Its section 9.1 names "distilled-indexer recall of
  oracle support" as the most direct next step, still undone as of 2026-10-08
  (C01, C38).
- Production placement matches the 4B layout. QSA puts its indexer on the
  softmax layers, "every fourth in this hybrid" (C04). On Qwen3.5-4B exact
  retrieval survives only through attention (C12), so an indexer on the eight
  softmax layers sits exactly where the model retrieves.
- Hybrid multilinguality is organized by layer. Qwen3.5 is less
  cross-lingually aligned than Qwen3 in 10 of 13 languages, and the largest
  cross-lingual event is at the first full-attention layer (C08). The 4B
  target's own MN-minus-CX gap is concentrated at layers 3 and 31 (E5). This
  is why v3 adds per-layer reporting and a per-layer veto on NEGATIVE.
- Program fit, stated plainly. Q3 is backfill behind Q1 and Q2; Q1 is closed
  (D46). K1 v3's projected caps, 7.21 to 9.79 GPU-h, are about K1 v2's 8.05
  and five to six times the Q3 file's original K1 estimate of 1.5 GPU-h. The
  H2 gate is the likeliest stop, and it ends the screen after about 1.1 to 1.6
  GPU-h used.
- Why now: the dense pre-check gave the go-ahead to design (D26 asked for it),
  and the lane-862 code path is the only measured 4B evaluation path. The
  window is not urgent: no concurrent paper runs the measurement (C38, C11).

   (masked LEX xi_rel at most 0.05). Masking turns the MN question's
   same-language haystack matches into distractors that a literal-leaning

   therefore go either way, so the registered gate is two-sided: |xi| at most

The three closest works:

1. **Oracle-Guided Sparse Prefill** (https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07703 , Wang
   et al., v1 2026-06-05) is the closest mechanism and the prior used for
   blind discrimination.
   - Shared with K1 v3: a frozen backbone, here including Qwen3.5 hybrid
     full-attention layers; a reference top-k selector built from dense
     attention mass; a learned selector KL-distilled to the same distribution;
     and the separation of selector error from what the budget alone loses.
   - Delta: K1 v3 measures per-prompt evidence-selection recall, not task
     quality. Its contrast is cross-script against same-language questions,
     as a difference-in-differences against the selector's own target, on
     overlap-masked evidence and entity-controlled questions, split by whether
     indexer training saw the script. It adds literal, random and null
     references and dense headroom and answering gates.
   - The paper's own section 9.1 names the monolingual version of this
     measurement (indexer recall of oracle support) as its next step. The
     novelty refuter in K1 v2 used exactly that to argue trivial recombination
     (about 0.6), and the argument is stronger now that the base is a
     Qwen3.5 hybrid.
   - Anonymized mechanism paragraphs are in the bundle (`blind/`).
2. **SpotAttention** (https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22874 , Ahmad and Yun, v1
   2026-06-22) is the direct apparatus prior on this exact base.
   - What it does: a KL-distilled selector on every full-attention layer of
     frozen Qwen3.5-4B, trained on English only, with parity at static budgets
     of 50 and 75 percent.
   - Delta: K1 v3's object is the selector's recall against its own target
     across scripts at a 12.5 percent budget. SpotAttention measures task
     parity in English at 50 to 75 percent.
   - Its "non-English data is untested" is the gap. Its English-only training
     stream would be the natural unseen-language baseline arm, which v3 does
     not register (decision 72).
   - A supplementary blind packet is in the bundle.
3. **Lost in Compression** (https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26175 , Lukauskas, v1
   2026-07-27) is the closest cross-lingual selector audit.
   - What it does: Belebele in ten languages, budgets matched in the target
     tokenizer, out-of-model compressors, questions always in the context's
     language.
   - Delta: K1 v3's selector is inside the model and is judged against its own
     dense attention, with question and needle languages differing.
   - Its rate dependence and its finding that the gap follows supervision
     language predict a small xi for K1's multilingually trained indexers.

## Mechanism and Falsifiable Predictions

Mechanism statement. This is the paragraph given to the blind critic,
anonymized (`blind/` in the bundle):

A frozen pretrained decoder language model whose layers alternate between a
fixed-size recurrent state and, at every fourth layer, softmax attention is
fitted at each softmax-attention layer with a small learned selector that
scores four-token blocks of the context. The selector uses a few query
projections of the current token, one pooled key per block and a gated sum of
rectified dot products, and it is trained only by minimizing the KL divergence
between its block-score distribution and a block aggregation of that layer's
own attention (head-summed mass or within-block maximum); the backbone
receives no gradient. Its training text includes some writing systems and
excludes others. At a fixed budget of 1,024 of 8,192 context tokens, the
selector's recall of a known evidence passage is compared with the recall of
the exact top blocks of its own training target. The quantity of interest is a
difference-in-differences: how much more evidence recall the selector loses
than its target when the question is a human translation in a different
writing system from the passage, compared with a question in the passage's own
language. Recall is counted only on passage blocks that share no content token
with either question, and only for questions without named-entity or number
anchors, so literal matching cannot favour either side; a purely literal
selector is run to check that this holds in both directions. The contrast is
estimated for writing systems absent from the selector's training and,
separately, for writing systems present in it, with a romanized
transliteration of the same passage and same-script translated questions as
descriptive comparators. Pre-registered gates require the dense model to show
selection headroom on the masked passage and to answer better with the passage
than without it before any difference is interpreted, and a negative result
also requires the selector to keep at least half of its target's
same-language headroom and no single layer or translation direction to show a
large excess.

Hypothesis under test (H_loc). A low-rank selector fitted to aggregated
attention reproduces its target's ranking where question and passage share
language. It fails to reproduce the target's non-literal cross-script
matching, so xi^M_T is greater than 0. The null is xi^M_T = 0: the selector
inherits the target's cross-script gap and adds nothing to it.

Three mechanisms could produce xi greater than 0. The design separates them
descriptively, never by a decision rule.

- M1, capacity or subspace: four 128-dimensional query heads cannot carry the
  part of a 16-head, 256-dimensional attention that does cross-script
  matching. Predicts an excess in both strata (label SEEN_TOO).
- M2, input-distribution shift: query rows or keys in a script absent from
  indexer training are out of distribution for its projections. Predicts an
  excess in the unseen stratum only (label UNSEEN_SPECIFIC). The TR leg moves
  bn's needle from the unseen Bengali script into Latin, which training saw,
  at fixed language and content. It is the key-side test.
- M3, layer position, new for the hybrid: cross-lingual matching concentrates
  at specific softmax layers (C08, E5). Predicts a layer-concentrated excess,
  which the per-layer table and the NEGATIVE veto expose.

Registered predictions and decision rules (draft registration, "Decision
rules"; thresholds fixed before any audit read):

- P1, GO (H_loc supported). For some target T:
  - xi^M_T at least 10, with the combined 99 percent lower bounds of xi^M_T and
    xi_rel^M_T both above 0;
  - V1 to V4 pass, and H1^M is at least 10;
  - the development pre-step and the H2 gate have passed.
  Labels from the seen stratum, CS and TR are descriptive.
- P0, NEGATIVE: the falsifier and kill criterion for H_loc at 4B. Every
  condition below must hold for both targets:
  - xi^M_T at most 5, with upper bound below 10 and half-width at most 5;
  - xi_rel^M_T at most 0.1, with upper bound below 0.2;
  - the non-literal floor: the 99 percent lower bounds of controlled G^M(MN)
    and of controlled G(MN) both at least 0.5;
  - V1 to V4 pass and H1^M is at least 20;
  - robustness: the seen stratum's point at most 5, each direction's point at
    most 5, and no layer with a point of at least 10 and a lower bound above 0.
  The Q3 kill criterion: publish the localization negative and stop.
- STOP_INSTRUMENT, at the development pre-step. Any of:
  - masked headroom fails;
  - the two-sided literal check fails;
  - the null is not CENTRED at two reaching sigmas, or the floor is not viable;
  - fewer than two seen languages survive;
  - too many families are lost to masking.
  The v3 id ends.
- UNINTERPRETABLE: H2a or H2b fails at the H2 gate (before any indexer is
  trained), or H1^M is below 10 on the audit read.
- HOLD: the seed-mean English ML excess of indexer over target has a 99
  percent lower bound above +1 point. Terminal (D16).
- VOID: integrity failure. INCONCLUSIVE: everything else, including the
  NULL_OFF_CENTRE, SEEN_EXCESS, DIRECTION_CONCENTRATED and LAYER_CONCENTRATED
  labels.

How K1 v2's falsifier defects are repaired:

1. **A flat selector inside NEGATIVE.** An indexer at random on both
   non-literal legs has G(MN) = 0. NEGATIVE now requires the 99 percent lower
   bound of G(MN) to be at least 0.5, on both unmasked and masked recall. The
   masked floor also fails a literal-only selector (masked G near 0; unmasked
   0.237, E3).
2. **NEGATIVE unreachable.** Development masked headroom must be at least 20
   with a lower bound of at least 10 before any indexer is trained. Unmasked
   headroom is 42.15 (E1); masking will lower it by an unmeasured amount
   (residual risk R2 below).
3. **GO confounded by literal anchors and paraphrase overlap.** The mask
   removes every shared content token from the measured evidence on both legs;
   entity-anchored questions are excluded; the literal selector must read
   within ±2.5 points and ±0.05 on the masked statistic. Under a linear-mixture
   reading, the unmasked floor caps a selector's literal share at 0.66, so the
   gate bounds the literal contribution to xi at about 1.6 points. Unmasked,
   it is about 6.3 (synthesis arithmetic in `syn/v3_power.py`).
4. **Equal selector noise through a top-k threshold.** The block-score null
   must be centred on the masked statistic at two or more sigmas near the V1
   boundary (development, 17-sigma grid) and again on the audit read (V4, for
   GO and NEGATIVE alike). The single-sigma margins of E3 cannot carry a
   verdict.
5. **Script exposure collinear with script.** The seen stratum (th, hi, km;
   seen in the same bilingual configuration in training) and the bn TR leg
   vary exposure at fixed cross-script status, and fixed language and content
   respectively. Language-level contrasts stay descriptive (C32: three seen
   languages cannot support an inferential claim about seen scripts).

Residual identification threats, stated plainly:

- R1. Masking turns same-language haystack matches into budget competitors on
  MN only. A selector more literal than its target is then penalised on MN,
  which biases xi downward, toward NEGATIVE. The two-sided literal gate bounds
  this for a pure literal selector. A partly literal indexer is bounded only
  under the linear-mixture reading, and top-k recall is not linear.
- R2. Masking changes the estimand to "excess loss on needle content that does
  not literally match either question". It may cut the target's headroom,
  because dense attention concentrates on literal matches, especially for
  English needles of about 100 tokens. The pre-step measures both.
- R3. Seen and unseen strata differ in more than exposure: Thai and Khmer have
  no word spacing, and km's training share was 11.7 percent short (E10). The
  fertility-matched contrasts are descriptive.
- R4. The TR needle is a Latin island in a native-script haystack, so its
  salience is a script feature. The indexer-minus-target difference cancels
  only the salience the two share. Latin is not script-neutral inside the
  model (C28).
- R5. Weak alignment only: the haystack is always in the needle's language. A
  NEGATIVE does not cover distractors in the question's language (C39).
- R6. Base-model literal priming acts on the target too (C36). The
  difference-in-differences cancels only the shared part.

Outcome map at 4B. These ranges are the synthesis owner's judgement from E1 to
E8 and C06, C07 and C14, not measurements. They are written down so the
reviewers can dispute them.

- STOP_INSTRUMENT at the development pre-step: about 0.15 to 0.3. The main
  sources are masked headroom, the two-sided literal check and km's survival.
- UNINTERPRETABLE at the H2 gate, given the pre-step passes: about 0.25 to 0.4.
  P(pass) is 0.69 at the development point of 8.6, and lower if the true
  effect is smaller (dev interval −9.3 to 26.4).
- Given both gates pass:
  - V1 extension called: about 0.2 to 0.4 (indexer 2.5 times larger, 1
    percent of DSA's warm-up tokens);
  - HOLD: at most about 0.05 (statistical trigger);
  - GO: about 0.15 to 0.3 (C06, C07 and C14 point to small effects; Stage-1
    indexers and unseen scripts point the other way);
  - NEGATIVE: about 0.35 to 0.55 (needs a cluster SD of the excess at most
    about 13 points; E2 implies about 6 for the literal selector);
  - INCONCLUSIVE for the remainder.
- Unconditionally, P(GO or NEGATIVE) is about 0.2 to 0.5.
