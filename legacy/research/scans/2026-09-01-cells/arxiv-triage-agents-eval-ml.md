## Occupancy shifts since 2026-08-10 (this group's columns)

1. **Automated harness evolution / harness search is now a crowded axis.** HarnessLens (+7.6-13.6% held-out, attribution-gated verification), StarHarness (+20-35 pp, transfers across GPT and Qwen without re-evolution), PILOT (live steering + live self-evolution; Terminal-Bench 2.0 up to +9.8 pp), OpsHarness (59.0% top-1 RCA, dual-gate admission), VideoHarness-RSI (harness search around frozen VLMs), Aspire (benchmark; weight-level self-evolution sparse and unstable, evolved harness below engineered reference). The 2026-08-10 program already rejected the universal-sidecar harness claim; this month adds *cross-model-family harness portability* (StarHarness) as an occupied claim.
2. **Agent memory / skill admission gating is occupied** (AdmitOR calibrated-FDR gate; Recuris validation-gated skill memory with failure localisation; OpsHarness dual gate; HarnessLens attributable-evidence gate). Dir 17 survives only as randomised identification; "learned memory gate" alone no longer discriminates.
3. **Byte-level compression evaluated on machine translation, and externally supervised byte-chunk boundaries, are occupied** (Autocompleting Tokenizers; When Tokenizers Fail). Dir 18 must show a delta over both.
4. **Multilingual tokenizer-fertility confounds are now documented and quantified** (Vowel Signs: 17/17 abugidas, 1.47x-9.02x floor, 4.43% BPB at equal compute, 63.3% of top HF repos affected; Arkios: MC-letter format at chance for a 1B bilingual model). These become mandatory controls in dir 18 baselines.
5. **Nested / sliceable byte-level vocabularies: pre-registered negative** (2608.28151). Do not re-propose one-model-many-granularities without addressing the -3.64% / -2.96% BPB gaps; the robustness side effect (12.5-15.4 pts less degradation under noise) belongs to multi-granularity training, not conditioning.
6. **Harness-RL with structured credit assignment is occupied** (Harness-RL CAPO action/args gradient routing; AgenticRag-R1 stack memory; River verifier-quality recipe). No CoTCodec direction targets it.
7. **Context-manager medium: pixels as history carrier is occupied** (VERA, -31.5 to -63.1% non-cache tokens, training-free). Relevant if a "reasoning media" direction is ever proposed; currently none is.
8. **Routing among test-time reasoning operators is weakened** (Recursive Agentic Reasoning: BRANCH dominant in 12/14 settings; gains correlate r = 0.72 with truncation). Any operator-router claim in the program must control for budget-truncation recovery.
9. **Evaluation protocol shifts to adopt**: model + harness as the tested solver (Same Model, Different Harness: F2PF 28% -> 49% from a mechanical context policy alone); paired passes with token/dollar accounting (Ledger: conditional multi-agent gains, Qwen3.6-35B -1 to -9); paired scoring and pipeline-failure separation (Recursive Agentic Reasoning); provenance-sensitive grading and all-k-of-k reliability (APIFlow-Bench: 7-pt best-of-5 spread vs 44-pt reliability spread; 77% of failures at delivery); completeness vs partial credit and false-completion rates (FrontierChallenge: 75.5% of failing Claude Code runs claim completion); semantic drift units for reproduction (SA-Bench 0.301 best).
10. **Production attention occupancy grows**: Motif 3 adds Grouped Differential Latent Attention, manifold-constrained hyper-connections, Expert-Specific PolyNorm and MTP at 314B/13.2B-active, 12.5T tokens. Belongs in the "Production linear hybrids / static mixtures" rows of the occupied table.
11. **LM-head gradient bottleneck**: causal negative (backward-only half-rank +0.0586 loss vs factorised head +0.1795). The Godey & Artzi "harmful bottleneck" claim should not be cited as established in any CoTCodec proposal.
