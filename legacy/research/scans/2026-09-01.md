reasoning media, test-time learning, byte/multilingual compute, diffusion LMs),
plus the agent-harness, local-model, Tinker, benchmark, and infrastructure

## Executive Summary

- 226 arXiv entries since 2026-08-10 across 15 topical queries; ~600 abstract
  pages and ~40 full texts opened; 22 deduplicated open gaps; 8 kill-shot
  verdicts on current directions.
- **Generic sequence-operator design is closed.** Three labs independently ship
  3 linear : 1 global hybrids (Kimi K3: 69 KDA + 24 gated MLA; Qwen3.8-Next:
  12 × (3 GDN → 1 Qwen Sparse Attention); GLM-5.3-Flash: 34 KDA + 11 DSA), with
  gated/hyper-connection residuals, learned sparse indexers, MTP heads, and
  learned sinks all standard. Delta-rule gate geometry (QED, SANE, MARCH,
  Falcon rules), operator routing (LoGo, Switch Attention, RouteSparse), and
  hybrid serving state algebra (DASC, Tail-Replay, TreeWY, DeltaLog) filled in
  during August alone.
- **The one region every cell found empty:** language/script as a controlled
  variable *inside* architecture — recurrent-state recall with content held
  fixed across translations, indexer selection across translations,
  parallel-data-supervised byte boundaries, translation-equivariant abstract
  reasoning codes, cross-lingual fast-weight readout. This is where production
  parallel translation data is the defining input.
- **Portability after PorTAL:** occupied among softmax transformers (incl. MoE,
  local/global attention, multimodal, cross-tokenizer distillation) and for
  "frozen object + thin reader" (Engram reader transfer, KV translation). Not
  found: porting a task adapter onto a different operator family, label-free
  base alignment, or porting an update rule.
- Kill-shots: D16 NARROWED (update-rule portability only); D17 NARROWED
  (Hindsight Memory-PRM takes post-hoc deletion credit; randomized
  identification survives); D18 STILL_OPEN (five new control arms required);
  Coded Delta → negative-result cell only; Bidirectional Plan Repair novelty
  collision with CID; Rollout-Value Operator Scheduling and Rank-Adaptive Edit
  Summaries largely occupied.
- Repo-facing corrections: `tinker==0.23.3` pin is stale (PyPI 0.27.0; the service rejects unsupported SDKs with HTTP 400 — observed for 0.22.3 in June 2026; whether 0.23.3 is still accepted cannot be checked without a key);
  32K Kimi context assumption overflows 60.6% of SWE-Bench episodes in the

## High-Priority Signals (score 4-5)

### 1. [On the Design of Qwen3.8-Next Architecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30320)
- **Source:** lab (Qwen) · **Thread:** F, E · **Variable(s):** architecture context for all
- **Score:** 5 · **Published:** 2026-08-31
- **Key finding:** GDN hybrid 53.81 vs SWA hybrid 51.15 vs full attention 49.87 (25B-A3B, 9-benchmark avg)†; sparse attention swapped in for the 1-in-4 global layers at continued pretraining; NoPE dropped after "substantially higher rate of endless generation after post-training"; loss and downstream accuracy do not always co-move.
- **Impact on CoTCodec:** the most detailed public hybrid ablation set; defines the 2026 baseline stack for any small-hybrid experiment; the NoPE disagreement with Kimi/Solar is an open mechanism (gap G10).
- **Action:** cite; update-design · **Cite key:** qwen2026next

### 2. [Kimi K3 technical report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24653) + [Attention Residuals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15031)
- **Source:** lab (Moonshot) · **Thread:** F · **Score:** 4 · **Published:** 2026-07-27 (v2 08-07)
- **Key finding:** 2.8T/104B-active, 69 KDA + 24 gated MLA, NoPE global layers, Block AttnRes, MTP-as-draft; "≈2.5× scaling efficiency vs K2" (joint, no per-component attribution)†; Block AttnRes matches a baseline with 1.25× compute†.
- **Impact:** no open ≤1B reference of this stack exists (gap G12); K3 weights (1.5+ TB) are not a local object; Kimi-Linear-48B-A3B-Base remains the only KDA base.
- **Action:** cite; add-baseline (small reference) · **Cite key:** kimi2026k3

## Landscape Summary

Between 2026-08-10 and 2026-09-01 the production frontier converged on one
hybrid recipe and filled the remaining operator-level gaps at preprint speed.
What did not fill is anything that treats language or script as a controlled
variable inside the architecture, anything that ports adaptation across operator
families or ports an update rule, and the methodology of causality-verified
recall audits at small scale. Direction 18 is the only current contract whose
mechanism remains unclaimed; D16 and D17 survive as narrowed deltas; the rest of
the ranked portfolio should be parked or run as negatives.

Coverage limits (full list in `2026-09-01-cells/synthesis.md` §5): WebSearch
budget exhausted mid-sweep; arXiv API, Semantic Scholar, and anonymous Jina
blocked from this network (arXiv and Jina worked only via the H100 host);
OpenReview, ACL Anthology, patents, Reddit/HN, live X, Chinese-language sources,
talks, and citation graphs were not covered by the cells (the verification pass
addresses venues, patents, and community replication); nothing was executed on
the H100 host or Tinker; the parallel translation corpus was never inventoried
because none exists in this repository.
